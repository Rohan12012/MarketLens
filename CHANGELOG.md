# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — November 5, 2026

- Portfolio analysis with ticker-prefix search, adjusted-price history, risk metrics,
  benchmark comparisons, and CSV exports.
- A 72-case concentration/rebalancing study with buy-and-hold, monthly, and daily
  strategies, turnover, and trading fees.
- SQLite storage, baseline joins, window-function rankings, findings, and an
  interactive Research study page.
- A trading-calendar audit, reconciled exports, rebuild checks, and a timestamped
  Validation tab that detects stale evidence.
- One-command study setup, locked development dependencies, automated tests,
  an executive summary, and an interview walkthrough.

Independent price verification remains open. The recorded secondary-source probe
returned a browser-verification page rather than comparable prices. Results are
historical model outputs, not a production trading or investment-advice service.
No hosted deployment is included in this release.
